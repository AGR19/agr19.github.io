Presented our paper on the GEMR:KG (Global Emerging Market Risk Knowledge Graph) at the 19th IADIS International Conference on Information Systems in Zagreb, Croatia. Aligning with the World Bank and IFC, we harness the power of knowledge graphs to handle the heterogeneity involved in complex economic datasets -- piloting a unified semantic model to track how financial distress and systemic risks can be analyzed across emerging countries.

Co-authors: Hemakshi Pandey, Hitesh Kolluru, Lalitha Shreya Vanam, Likitha Sathvik Potineni, and Dr. Srividya Bansal.
